See your Life X-ray

Stand in front of your camera and watch your skeleton, muscles, heart and organs follow your body in 3D. A pose recognition model names the pose you hold, scores your form and lights up the muscles that are working.

Best results: stand 1.5–2.5 m from the camera so your whole upper body (or whole body) is in the picture, with light in front of you. This is an educational visual overlay, not a medical or diagnostic tool.

    See your Life X-ray tracks 33 body points and 21 points on each hand with MediaPipe BlazePose. A Python server (FastAPI, scikit-learn) turns the body points into 76 pose features and a classifier trained for this project recognises the pose, scores the form and reports the working muscles. A 3D anatomy dataset of one adult is split into 45 body segments and bent to follow you every frame.

    Educational visual overlay. It shows where structures typically sit in an average adult body, scaled to you. It is not a scan of you and not a medical or diagnostic tool. The heartbeat animation is an illustration, not your pulse.
